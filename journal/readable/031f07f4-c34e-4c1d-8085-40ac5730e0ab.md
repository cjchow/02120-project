## What you learned today (additions)

**Symlog axes**
- Linear near 0, then a real base-10 log scale past the cutoff (1 year for us).
- A pure log axis can't show t = 0, because log(0) doesn't exist.

**numpy slicing**
- `web[i]` is one row: all three levels at one moment.
- `web[:, 0]` is one column: producers over all time.
- `web[:0]` (no comma) is an **empty** slice. That's a sneaky bug.

**Dinosaur facts**
