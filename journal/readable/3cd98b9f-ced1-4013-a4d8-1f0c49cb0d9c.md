**Lesson: what "lifetime" means (point 1 of a few)**

1. In [main.py:30](project/folder2/main.py#L30), each haze is `tau * exp(-t / lifetime)`. Soot uses `t_soot` and sulfate uses `t_sulf`.
2. The formula means that **every year, the same *fraction* of the haze rains out**. It's not the same *amount*. Thick haze loses a lot, and thin haze loses only a little.
3. The **lifetime** is the time it takes for the haze to drop to **1/e ≈ 37%** of where it started. (e ≈ 2.718 is the same e from `exp(-tau)` last session.)
4. So a short lifetime means the haze clears fast, and a long lifetime means it lingers.

**Claude:** **Your question first: yes.** In this model, `t_soot` is the soot's residence time, and "lifetime" means the same thing.
