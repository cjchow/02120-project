### What you learned today
- **Units:** temperatures are in K, and heat flows are in W/m². Never mix them in one subtraction.
- **The drain** = `EPS × SIGMA × Ts⁴`. It's infrared heat lost to space for good, and it grows fast with temperature (2× the temperature → 16× the drain).
- **Equilibrium:** faucet = drain, so `net = 0`. At T0 both are 238 W/m².
- **EPS = ABSORBED / (SIGMA × T0⁴) = 0.6106.** It's tuned so the model balances at T0. You derived it yourself.
- **The thermostat:** a colder surface has a smaller drain, so `net > 0` and it warms back toward T0. The reverse happens if it's too hot.
- **Hand check:** with no aerosols and both tanks starting at T0, `net = 0` and `exch = 0` every day, so `Ts` stays at exactly 288 K.
- **`exch = GAMMA × (Ts − Td)`** is a hose between the tanks, not a drain. Positive means heat flows down, and negative means it flows up. The deep ocean cushions the surface's cooling.
- **Total heat the surface gains = `net − exch`.**
